Central Idea & Theme

Passage

Every Saturday, Maya organized a neighborhood cleanup. At first, only her brother joined her.

By spring, many neighbors were helping. Their small, steady efforts had made the park welcoming again.

Correct Answer: B) Consistent effort can inspire a community to improve its surroundings.

Iko's Quick Read

The passage follows a small effort that grows into a community project.

Step-by-step solution

  1. Maya starts with one helper.
  2. Her steady work inspires neighbors; B captures the whole passage.

Why the other choices don't work

  • A

    The work happens every Saturday, not only in spring.

  • C

    One brother does not support a claim about all brothers.

  • D

    The passage does not mention a large budget.

Concept tip

A central idea should account for the entire passage.
