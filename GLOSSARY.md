# SHSAT learning and content

Shared teaching vocabulary for the student experience, teacher workspace, and Content Editor. These terms describe the intended learning model, independently of how the application stores it.

## Content

**Question bank**: The organizational home of reusable authored questions. The five banks are Reading Comprehension, Math, Revising/Editing A, Stand Alone Items, and SHSAT Lab Questions.

**Source**: The publisher or original material from which content came, such as Official Handbook, Prestige, Kaplan, or SHSAT Lab. Source remains distinct from the worksheets and assessments that use the content.
_Avoid_: Category when referring to a publisher.

**Educational area**: The kind of SHSAT work: Reading Comprehension, Math, Revising/Editing A, or Stand Alone Items. A SHSAT Lab question also belongs to the appropriate educational area.

**Category**: A broad instructional grouping within an educational area's classification system. A category does not describe a source or a teacher-curated collection.

**Topic**: A more specific instructional focus within a category, where that classification system needs another level.

**Math skill**: A specific, teacher-managed mathematical capability within a topic. It is distinct from difficulty and optional diagnostic annotations.
_Avoid_: Skilled as a student-facing noun.

**Passage**: A shared text or stimulus needed to answer associated questions. Its genre and presentation format are distinct from the skills tested by its questions.

**Question version**: A particular edition of a question, its answer key, explanation, and necessary passage or assets. Historical student evidence refers to the edition the student encountered.

**Question usage**: An appearance of a question in a worksheet or assessment, or an explicit relationship to a practice generator. One question can have several usages without having several independently editable copies.

## Learning activities

**Worksheet**: A finite, ordered collection of questions for instruction and practice, managed separately from assessments.

**Focused worksheet**: A worksheet intended to practice one declared instructional focus.

**Mixed-review worksheet**: A worksheet intentionally covering several instructional focuses within its declared scope. Its questions retain their individual classifications.
_Avoid_: General as a substitute for the questions' actual classifications.

**Collection**: A teacher-curated grouping of learning activities, such as a recommended sequence. Membership organizes discovery without changing an activity's identity.

**Assessment**: A finite collection of questions used to evaluate performance under specified conditions. An exam is a form of assessment.

**Practice generator**: A reviewed rule or template that produces question variations within a defined domain. Its source questions describe its provenance, not its generated output's identity.

**Practice module**: A student-facing activity combining an instructional focus with an available set of question generators or an explicitly identified finite question pool.

**Infinite practice**: Repeatable practice that produces question variations. The name does not promise mathematically unlimited unique questions or describe an ordinary finite question pool.

**Generated question instance**: The exact question variation presented to a student, together with the answer and context necessary to interpret that response later.

**Study Hall**: The student destination for finding optional learning activities by subject and instructional focus. Assignment to a student is a separate decision from availability in Study Hall.

## Delivery and evidence

**Publication**: Making a validated version available for its intended audience. Publication is separate from assigning work or opening an assessment session.

**Publication intent**: A teacher's saved request to publish a future generator/module after validation and approval. It is separate from planner build status and does not establish current student availability.

**Assignment**: Delivery of a particular activity to a student or class with a due date, completion requirement, and applicable settings.

**Attempt**: One student's run of an assigned or self-directed activity. Resuming continues that attempt; a retake creates another attempt.

**Response**: A student's recorded answer to a particular authored question version or generated instance within an attempt.

**Correction**: Work performed after a response to explain or repair an error. A correction does not replace the original response or grade.

**Prior exposure**: Evidence that a student encountered the same authored question or generated instance before the current response. Practice on the same generator family is a separate, broader form of familiarity.

**Coverage**: The instructional areas represented by content or student evidence. The presence of a category or module alone does not establish usable content or demonstrated learning.

**Readiness**: Whether the content, classification, rendering, grading, publication, and access conditions required for a stated use have been satisfied.
